PALWARDENS v1.4 · CRT-14

Pets evolve from baby to toddler in about 10 minutes, toddler to teen in 3 hours, and teen to adult in a day. Your pet keeps ageing while the screen is off.

Each evolution offers 3 elements. Two elements fuse into a new type, and the adult picks a material that sets rarity, attack and defence.

Once every pet is grown up, you can hatch another egg (up to 4 pets). Grown-ups look after themselves and wander in the distance. Use the ⇄ button on the monitor to switch between them.

Test mode is for building and testing: it gives 9999 coins and adds an "Evolve now" button. Turning it off puts your real coins back.
